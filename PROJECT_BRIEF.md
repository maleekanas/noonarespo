# PROJECT_BRIEF.md

## Project Title
Arabic Kids Academy

## Project Vision
Build a world-class online Arabic language learning platform for children that combines modern education, AI-powered personalization, parent engagement, administrative automation, and financial management into a single integrated ecosystem.
The platform should enable children worldwide to learn Arabic through live classes, self-paced learning, gamification, interactive activities, and AI tutors while giving parents complete visibility into their child's educational journey.

---

## Executive Summary
Arabic Kids Academy is a cloud-based educational platform designed for children aged 4-16 years old.
The platform will support:
- Arabic Language Learning
- Reading & Writing Skills
- Speaking & Listening Practice
- Quran & Tajweed Programs
- Islamic Studies
- Interactive Learning Activities
- AI-Powered Tutoring
- Parent Engagement
- School Administration
- Teacher Management
- Financial Operations
- Student Performance Analytics

The platform must provide an engaging, safe, and scalable learning environment for students, parents, teachers, and administrators.

## Academic Management System

### Learning Programs
1. **Arabic Foundations**: Alphabet Recognition, Letter Sounds, Vocabulary Building, Reading Practice
2. **Reading Program**: Phonics, Reading Fluency, Reading Comprehension, Story-Based Learning
3. **Writing Program**: Handwriting, Sentence Formation, Creative Writing, Grammar Practice
4. **Speaking Program**: Pronunciation, Conversation Practice, Public Speaking
5. **Listening Program**: Story Listening, Audio Exercises, Interactive Comprehension
6. **Quran Program**: Quran Reading, Memorization, Tajweed, Revision
7. **Islamic Studies**: Basic Islamic Knowledge, Prophetic Stories, Islamic Values

### Curriculum Management
- Curriculum Builder
- Lesson Planning
- Learning Objectives
- Progress Mapping
- Learning Outcomes Tracking

### Course Management
- Create Courses & Levels
- Course Scheduling & Pricing
- Enrollment Management

### Class Management
- Live Virtual Classes & Recorded Sessions
- Interactive Whiteboard, Screen Sharing, Classroom Chat, File Sharing
- Video Integration: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet
